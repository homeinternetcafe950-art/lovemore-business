Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Lovemore Business Enterprise ("we", "us", "our") is based in Hammanskraal, Pretoria, South Africa. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or request a quote, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you reach out via our contact form, WhatsApp, phone, or email, we may collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • Details about the service or project you're enquiring about
  • Any files, images, or reference material you send us for a design or print job

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Communicate with you about your order, from confirmation to collection
  • Keep basic records for invoicing and job tracking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp Communication

Our quote form and WhatsApp buttons open a chat via WhatsApp. Messages sent this way are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices in addition to this policy. We keep WhatsApp conversation records only for as long as needed to complete your order and for basic customer service history.

Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the information you share with us secure, and we only use it for the purposes described above.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll action your request promptly.
